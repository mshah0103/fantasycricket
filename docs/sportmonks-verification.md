**Finding (13 of 13 match):** Sportmonks stores the two fielders in Cricinfo's order.
- `catch_stump_player_id` is the first-named fielder, normally the one who fielded and threw.
- `runout_by_id` is the second-named fielder, normally the one at the stumps who completed the run-out. This is not a fixed role. It was the keeper in 9 cases, the bowler of that delivery in 3, and another fielder (Shubman Gill) in 1. The keeper also appears first, as the thrower, in 3 cases (Buttler twice, Rahul once).
- With only one fielder, Sportmonks puts him in `catch_stump_player_id` and leaves `runout_by_id` null.

**Cricsheet does not keep this order.** For the same 2025 two-fielder run-outs, Cricsheet lists the fielders in the Sportmonks/Cricinfo order 9 times out of 19 and reversed 10 times. For the two m32 Super Over run-outs it names only one fielder where Sportmonks and Cricinfo name two. So Cricsheet's fielder order can't tell which fielder to credit. The Cricsheet import (2008–2012) therefore never attributes a multi-fielder run-out (see Spec impact 1).

## Spec impact

1. **§5.6 run-out credit (decision needed).** The spec credits "the `runoutby` relation", i.e. `runout_by_id`. That field is null on every single-fielder run-out, and on every run-out in 2013–2020. Under the spec as written, these are flagged `runout_fielder_missing` and nobody scores them:
   - 2025: 17 of 39 run-outs flagged. 2013–2020: all 486 flagged. 2013–2026 in total: see Gate 1 above.
   - The fielders who do get credit (two-fielder cases) are the ones at the stumps (keeper or bowler). The thrower gets nothing.
   - Options for the commissioner:
     - **(a) Keep as written.** Leaves every flagged run-out for the stat-correction tool (§12.3 gate 1 fallback).
     - **(b) Fall back to `catch_stump_player_id` when `runout_by_id` is null.** This clears nearly all flags (only 7 run-outs in 2013–2026 name no fielder at all). Two-fielder run-outs still credit the fielder at the stumps. This is a one-line change in `runOutCreditedFielder` (`packages/sportmonks/src/fieldMap.ts`). For Cricsheet years it credits single-fielder run-outs and keeps multi-fielder ones flagged.
     - **(c) Always credit the first-named fielder (`catch_stump_player_id`, normally the thrower).** This is consistent across all seasons, but it changes who scores two-fielder run-outs.
   - Whichever is chosen, §5.6's sentence "In Sportmonks this is the `runoutby` relation" should be updated to say which field is used and what happens when it is null.
2. **§5.8 Super Overs.**
   - Repeat Super Overs are missing from Sportmonks. 2020 match 36 (15267) had two, and only the first is in the data. This can't be detected from Sportmonks alone.
   - 2013–2017 Super Over ties have no Super Over batting, bowling or ball detail. They are flagged `ball_data_missing` at fixture level.
   - The fallback (commissioner override per fixture) stands. Cricsheet has Super Over innings for these matches and could fill them as a one-time patch. That is a possible follow-up, not in this plan.
3. **§7.2 positions.** Sportmonks has 8 position values, not 4: `Batsman`, `Bowler`, `Wicketkeeper`, `Allrounder`, `Top Order Batter`, `Middle Order Batter`, `Batting Allrounder` and `Bowling Allrounder`. The suggested mapping is Wicketkeeper → WK; Batsman, Top Order Batter and Middle Order Batter → BAT; Allrounder, Batting Allrounder and Bowling Allrounder → ALL; Bowler → BWL. This needs the commissioner's confirmation before M1's player sync. Positions are each player's current profile value, not historical (consistent with §12.2).
4. **§7.4 playing XI and §12.4 chance of playing.** `lineup[].lineup.substitution` marks all five named substitutes, not the Impact Player who actually comes on. Before he bats or bowls, the app can show the XI plus the five named substitutes, but not which one will be used. After the match, the used Impact Player can only be inferred from who batted or bowled. Gate 5 timing is still pending (live recordings).
5. **§5.5 economy / gate 4.** The bowling card's `wide` field is wide **runs**, not deliveries. When ball-by-ball data is missing, the card fallback can't give per-delivery wide or no-ball penalties. Those counts are left unknown and flagged `ball_data_missing`, as the spec's anti-goal requires. Card and ball data agree on 863 of 863 bowling rows in 2025. 2019 is the noisiest season, with 65 mismatches.
6. **§12.1 past seasons / gate 6.** Sportmonks has IPL 2013–2026 with full scorecards and ball-by-ball data. 2008–2012 are missing, so Task 12 imports them from Cricsheet. The 2015, 2017, 2024 and 2025 seasons each have fewer ball-by-ball fixtures than fixtures because of washouts. Those are not data gaps.
7. **§11 live statuses.** The documented live status values are `1st Innings`, `2nd Innings`, `Innings Break`, `Int.`, and so on. The archive holds only finished fixtures, so they are unconfirmed until the Big Bash recordings (gate 7). The fixture's `live` boolean is `true` on finished matches and must not be used.
